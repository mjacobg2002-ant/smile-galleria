Meet Your Dentist

Dr. Wilson J. Kim, DDS

Dr. Kim has been practicing dentistry since graduating from the Columbia University School of Dental & Oral Surgery in 2000. He earned his B.A. from Colgate University and served as a Captain in the U.S. Air Force before entering private practice in 2003, relocating to Virginia in 2006.

Fluent in both English and Korean and a father of three, Dr. Kim connects naturally with patients of every age. He believes the foundation of great dentistry is trust — taking the time to listen, educate, and make every visit comfortable.
