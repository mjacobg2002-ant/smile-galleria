Meet Your Dentist

Meet Wilson J. Kim, DDS

Experience, patience and care for the whole family.

Dr. Kim has practiced dentistry since 2000. After graduating from the Columbia University School of Dental & Oral Surgery — and earning his B.A. from Colgate University — he served as a dentist in the United States Air Force. He has practiced privately in Virginia since 2006.

Fluent in English and Korean and a father of three, Dr. Kim connects naturally with children and adults, building the trust and rapport that make comfortable care possible.
